Coil inductance handbook formulas \bigbreak
\normalsize

\tiny
(Units are millimeters) \bigbreak
\normalsize

Solenoid coils \smallbreak
Wheeler25: \enspace $\rm L_H = \frac{5N^2 R^2}{1143R + 1270H}$ \bigbreak

Planar coils \smallbreak
Terman43: \enspace  $\rm L_P = \frac{5N^2 R^2}{1016R + 1397W}$ \bigbreak

Inverse conical coil \smallbreak
$\rm{L = \sqrt{(L_H \sin\alpha)^2 + (L_P \cos\alpha)^2}}$ \bigbreak

\endcenter
Where N is number of turns,  $\alpha$ is slope angle, R is mean radius,
H is height, and W is the coil width that is a circle radius at the end
of the winding minus the radius at the beginning of the winding \medbreak

All measurements are from the center of the wire.
